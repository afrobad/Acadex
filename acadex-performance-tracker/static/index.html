Deterministic Classification Rules:
  • Safe: 0 danger zones. All assessed categories are at or above danger limits.
  • Warning: Exactly 1 danger zone triggered.
  • Critical: 2 or more danger zones triggered.
  • Pending parts are treated as not assessed and never trigger danger. Final-Term pending is excluded.
