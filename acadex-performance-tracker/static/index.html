⚠️ Danger Zone & Performance Standing Rules
Deterministic risk classification and destructive course operations.

Active Policy Thresholds:

  • Critical Standing: Triggered when Student Attendance is below threshold (default 60%) AND at least one academic marks category is below threshold (default 40%).
  • Warning Standing: Triggered when any individual component (attendance, quiz, assignment, presentation, or midterm) falls below threshold.
  • OK Standing: All assessed categories are at or above passing threshold.
